## Dialogs

| Action               | Hotkeys                 |
|----------------------|-------------------------|

| Align and Distribute | <kbd>Shift+Ctrl+A</kbd> |
| Export Bitmap        | <kbd>Shift+Ctrl+E</kbd> |
| Document Properties  | <kbd>Shift+Ctrl+D</kbd> |
| Text Properties      | <kbd>Shift+Ctrl+T</kbd> |
